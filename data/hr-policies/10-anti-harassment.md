# Anti-Harassment and Inclusion Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** People Operations & Legal

## Commitment

The company maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards harassment, discrimination, and bullying. This applies to all employees, contractors, interns, and visitors in any work-related context — in the office, remote, at client sites, at company events, and on work-related social media.

## What Constitutes Harassment

Harassment is unwanted conduct related to a protected characteristic (race, gender, gender identity, age, disability, religion, sexual orientation, nationality, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law) that has the purpose or effect of:

- Violating a person's dignity, or
- Creating an intimidating, hostile, degrading, humiliating, or offensive environment.

Examples include (but are not limited to): offensive jokes, derogatory comments, unwanted physical contact, persistent unwanted personal attention, exclusion from work activities, and inappropriate messages.

**Sexual harassment** includes unwanted sexual advances, requests for sexual favours, and any other verbal or physical conduct of a sexual nature.

## Reporting

Anyone who experiences or witnesses harassment should report it through any of the following channels:

1. **Direct report to manager** — if the manager is not involved in the incident.
2. **People Operations** — people@company.com or in person.
3. **Anonymous Ethics Hotline** — details available on the intranet. Anonymous reports will be investigated to the extent possible given available information.

There is no time limit on reporting, but prompt reporting helps ensure a thorough investigation.

## Investigation Process

All reports are taken seriously and investigated promptly, typically within 10 business days. Investigations are conducted by People Operations, with Legal involvement where appropriate. Both the complainant and the subject will have the opportunity to present their account. Confidentiality is maintained to the extent possible.

## Outcomes

If harassment is substantiated, outcomes may include formal warning, mandatory training, transfer, demotion, or termination, depending on severity.

## Non-Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who makes a good-faith report or participates in an investigation is a separate, serious violation of this policy and will be dealt with as such.

## Training

All employees complete anti-harassment training within their first 10 business days and annually thereafter. Managers receive additional bystander intervention and investigation training.
