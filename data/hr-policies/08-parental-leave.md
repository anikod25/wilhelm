# Parental Leave Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** People Operations

## Eligibility

All employees who have completed at least six months of continuous employment are eligible for parental leave. This policy applies to birth parents, non-birth parents, and adoptive parents equally.

## Entitlement

| Role | Duration | Pay |
|------|----------|-----|
| Primary caregiver | 16 weeks | 100% of base salary |
| Secondary caregiver | 4 weeks | 100% of base salary |

Employees may extend leave beyond these periods using accrued annual leave, unpaid leave, or a combination. Extended leave must be agreed with People Operations at least four weeks before the planned start date.

## Notification

Employees should notify their manager and People Operations as early as reasonably practicable, and no later than eight weeks before the intended leave start date. Written notice should include the expected start date and anticipated return date.

## Flexible Return

Employees returning from primary caregiver leave may request a phased return of up to four weeks (e.g., 3 days/week for two weeks, then 4 days/week for two weeks before returning to full hours). Requests must be made at least two weeks before the planned return date and are subject to operational feasibility.

## Job Protection

Employees on parental leave retain their role or an equivalent role at the same grade and compensation. Performance reviews during leave will not be penalised; employees will receive the average team rating for any review cycle occurring entirely during leave.

## Benefits During Leave

All benefits (health insurance, pension contributions, equity vesting) continue without interruption during paid parental leave.

## Keeping in Touch

Employees on parental leave may, entirely voluntarily, work up to 10 "Keeping in Touch" days without affecting their leave entitlement. These days cannot be required by the company.
