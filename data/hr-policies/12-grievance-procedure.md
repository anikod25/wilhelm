# Grievance Procedure

**Effective date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** People Operations

## Purpose

This procedure provides a fair and consistent process for employees to raise workplace concerns. All grievances are handled with confidentiality and without fear of retaliation.

## What Is a Grievance?

A grievance is a formal complaint about a workplace issue that has not been resolved informally. Examples include concerns about working conditions, application of company policies, interpersonal conflict, or perceived unfair treatment.

Matters covered by the Anti-Harassment Policy follow a separate investigation procedure.

## Step 1 — Informal Resolution (Recommended First Step)

Before raising a formal grievance, employees are encouraged to speak directly with the person involved or raise the matter with their manager. Many issues can be resolved quickly and informally. People Operations can provide coaching support at this stage on request.

## Step 2 — Formal Grievance

If the informal approach fails or is not appropriate, the employee submits a written grievance to People Operations using the Grievance Submission Form (available on the intranet). The form should describe:

- The nature of the grievance
- Key dates and facts
- Any individuals involved
- The outcome the employee is seeking

**Acknowledgement:** People Operations will acknowledge receipt within two business days.

## Step 3 — Investigation

A People Operations representative (or an appointed neutral investigator for complex cases) will:

- Meet with the employee to understand the grievance in full
- Interview relevant witnesses
- Review any relevant documentation

The investigation will be completed within 15 business days of acknowledgement, or the employee will be notified of a revised timeline.

## Step 4 — Outcome

The employee will receive a written outcome within five business days of the investigation concluding. The outcome will explain the findings and any actions the company will take.

## Step 5 — Appeal

If the employee is not satisfied with the outcome, they may appeal in writing to the Head of People Operations within 10 business days of receiving the outcome. The appeal will be reviewed by a senior leader who was not involved in the original investigation. The appeal outcome is final.

## Record Keeping

Grievance records are kept confidentially in the employee's HR file and are retained for five years after the employee's departure.
