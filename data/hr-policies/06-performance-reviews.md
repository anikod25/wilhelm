# Performance Review Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** People Operations

## Review Cycle

Performance reviews are conducted twice per year:

- **Mid-year review:** Completed in July. Primarily a development-focused check-in; no compensation changes result directly from this cycle.
- **Annual review:** Completed in December. Informs compensation adjustments, promotion decisions, and the following year's goal-setting.

New employees hired within three months of a review cycle are exempt from that cycle and will participate in the next one.

## Process

1. **Goal-setting:** At the start of each year (or within 30 days of hire), employees set 3–5 SMART goals in the HR portal in collaboration with their manager.
2. **Self-assessment:** Employees complete a self-assessment form in the HR portal at least five business days before their scheduled review meeting.
3. **Manager assessment:** Managers complete their assessment independently before the review meeting.
4. **Review meeting:** A 60-minute 1:1 between employee and manager to discuss performance, development areas, and goals.
5. **Calibration:** Managers participate in a calibration session with their peer group to ensure rating consistency before results are finalised.
6. **Outcome communication:** Final ratings and any compensation changes are communicated to employees within two weeks of calibration closing.

## Rating Scale

| Rating | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| 5 – Exceptional | Consistently exceeds all goals; significant impact beyond role scope |
| 4 – Exceeds expectations | Meets all goals and exceeds several; demonstrably above role level |
| 3 – Meets expectations | Meets all or most goals; fully effective in role |
| 2 – Partially meets expectations | Meets some goals; improvement needed in key areas |
| 1 – Does not meet expectations | Significant gaps; performance improvement plan required |

## Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)

Employees receiving a rating of 1 will be placed on a formal PIP within 10 business days of the review. A PIP document specifies measurable improvement targets, a timeline (typically 60–90 days), and the support the employee will receive. People Operations must be involved in all PIPs.

## Appeals

Employees may appeal their rating by submitting a written request to People Operations within 10 business days of receiving their review outcome. Appeals are reviewed by the employee's skip-level manager and a People Operations representative.
