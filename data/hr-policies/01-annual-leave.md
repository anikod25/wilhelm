# Annual Leave Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** People Operations

## Entitlement

All full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year (pro-rated for part-time employees based on contracted hours). Accrual begins on the first day of employment.

Employees in their first year accrue leave monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month.

## Requesting Leave

Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least five business days in advance for absences of one to three days, and at least fifteen business days in advance for absences of four or more consecutive days.

Requests are subject to manager approval and must consider team coverage requirements. Managers should respond within three business days of a request being submitted.

## Carry-Over

Up to 5 days of unused annual leave may be carried over into the following calendar year. Carried-over leave must be used before March 31 or it will lapse. No cash payment is made for lapsed leave.

## Leave on Termination

Upon termination of employment, accrued and unused annual leave will be paid out at the employee's current base daily rate.

## Public Holidays

Public holidays are in addition to annual leave entitlement. Where an employee is required to work on a public holiday, they will receive a day off in lieu to be taken within 60 days.
