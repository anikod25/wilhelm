# IT Acceptable Use and Security Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** IT & People Operations

## Scope

This policy governs the use of all company-owned or company-managed devices, networks, software, and data — including systems accessed remotely.

## Acceptable Use

Company technology resources are provided for business purposes. Incidental personal use is permitted provided it is brief, does not consume significant bandwidth, and does not involve any prohibited content or activities.

**Prohibited uses include:**

- Accessing, storing, or distributing illegal, obscene, or discriminatory content
- Installing unlicensed or unauthorised software
- Attempting to circumvent security controls or access systems without authorisation
- Using company resources to operate a personal business or for extended personal activities
- Sharing credentials with anyone, including colleagues

## Password and Authentication

- All accounts must use passwords of at least 14 characters or passphrases.
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company accounts.
- Passwords must not be reused across systems or shared via unencrypted channels.
- A company-approved password manager is provided to all employees at no cost.

## Device Security

- Company devices must have full-disk encryption enabled at all times.
- Automatic screen lock must be set to 5 minutes or less.
- Operating system and application updates must be applied within 72 hours of release.
- Employees must not disable or circumvent endpoint protection software.

## Data Handling

- Customer data, employee data, and intellectual property are classified as Confidential and must be stored only in approved systems.
- Confidential data must not be emailed to personal accounts or stored on personal devices or unapproved cloud services.
- When in doubt about data classification, consult the Data Classification Guide on the intranet.

## Incident Reporting

Suspected security incidents — including lost or stolen devices, phishing clicks, unusual account activity, or accidental data exposure — must be reported to the IT helpdesk (security@company.com) within one hour of discovery. Delays in reporting may be treated as a policy violation.

## Monitoring

The company reserves the right to monitor activity on company-owned systems and networks for security and compliance purposes. Employees have no expectation of privacy on company devices or networks.

## Consequences

Violations may result in suspension of access, disciplinary action, or termination.
