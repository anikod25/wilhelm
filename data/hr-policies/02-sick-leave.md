# Sick Leave Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** People Operations

## Entitlement

Full-time employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does not accrue — the full balance is available from January 1 each year (or from the date of hire for new joiners).

Unused sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out on termination.

## Notification

Employees must notify their manager by 9:00 AM on the first day of absence, or as early as reasonably practicable. Notification should be made by phone or messaging platform, not email alone.

## Medical Certification

For absences of three or more consecutive days, a medical certificate from a registered practitioner must be provided within two business days of returning to work. The company reserves the right to request a certificate for any absence where a pattern of short-term absences has been observed.

## Extended Illness

If an employee exhausts their paid sick leave entitlement, additional absence may be covered by:

1. Annual leave (at the employee's request)
2. Unpaid medical leave, approved by People Operations

Employees on extended medical leave of more than four consecutive weeks should contact People Operations to discuss return-to-work planning and any available accommodations.

## Return to Work

Following an absence of five or more consecutive days, employees are required to complete a return-to-work discussion with their manager before resuming full duties.
