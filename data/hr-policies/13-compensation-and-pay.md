# Compensation and Pay Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** People Operations & Finance

## Pay Cycle

Employees are paid on the last business day of each month via direct bank transfer. The payroll cut-off for changes (new hires, salary adjustments, terminations) is the 15th of each month. Changes received after the cut-off take effect in the following month's payroll.

## Salary Review

Salary reviews are conducted once per year, following the annual performance review cycle (December). Adjustments, if any, are effective from February 1 of the following year. A salary review does not guarantee an increase.

Factors considered in salary reviews include:
- Annual performance rating
- Position within the salary band for the role
- Internal equity
- External market benchmarking (conducted annually by People Operations)

## Salary Bands

Every role is assigned to a salary band based on job level and function. Bands are reviewed annually against market data. Employees may request to know their band by contacting People Operations. The company does not disclose individual salaries.

## Bonus Programme

Eligible employees (as defined in their employment contract) participate in an annual discretionary bonus programme. Target bonus amounts are expressed as a percentage of base salary by level. Actual payouts depend on company performance and individual performance ratings. Bonuses are paid in February, alongside the first payroll that reflects the salary review.

Employees must be actively employed on the bonus payment date to receive a payout. Pro-rata bonuses are not paid for partial years unless specified in the employment contract.

## Payslips

Digital payslips are available in the HR portal by the 25th of each month. Employees should review payslips promptly and raise any discrepancies with People Operations within 30 days.

## Pay Queries

Payroll queries should be directed to payroll@company.com. Urgent issues (e.g., a missing payment) are resolved within two business days.
