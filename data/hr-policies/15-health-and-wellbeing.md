# Health and Wellbeing Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** People Operations

## Commitment

The company is committed to supporting the physical, mental, and financial wellbeing of all employees. Wellbeing is not a perk — it is a business priority.

## Health Insurance

All full-time employees and their immediate dependants are enrolled in the company health insurance plan from their first day of employment. Plan details, including coverage levels, in-network providers, and how to make a claim, are available in the HR portal under Benefits.

Part-time employees working 20 or more hours per week are eligible for the individual (non-dependant) plan.

## Mental Health Support

- **Employee Assistance Programme (EAP):** All employees have access to the EAP, which provides free, confidential counselling (up to 8 sessions per issue per year), legal advice, and financial coaching. The EAP is available 24/7 and can be accessed directly — no referral or manager involvement needed.
- **Mental Health Days:** Employees may take up to 2 mental health days per year from their sick leave balance without providing a medical certificate.
- **Manager training:** All people managers receive mental health first-aid training within six months of taking on a management role.

## Wellbeing Allowance

Each employee receives a $600 annual wellbeing allowance to spend on activities or services that support their physical or mental health, including:
- Gym memberships or fitness classes
- Meditation or mindfulness apps
- Ergonomic home office equipment (if not already provided under the Remote Work Policy)
- Nutrition or sleep coaching

Claims are submitted via the HR portal with a receipt. The allowance is not transferable and does not roll over.

## Working Hours and Rest

- Standard full-time hours are 40 hours per week. Consistent overwork is a management concern, not a badge of honour.
- Employees are encouraged to take their full lunch break and to disconnect outside working hours.
- Managers are expected to model healthy working habits and to address workload concerns raised by their team promptly.
- Employees experiencing persistent workload issues should raise them with their manager, skip-level, or People Operations.

## Pregnancy and Fertility

The company covers reasonable costs for fertility treatment not covered by the health insurance plan, up to $5,000 lifetime, through the EAP's specialist financial support service. Applications are made directly through the EAP and are completely confidential from the company.

## Review

This policy is reviewed annually in January by People Operations in consultation with employee feedback collected during the prior year's engagement survey.
