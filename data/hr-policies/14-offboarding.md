# Offboarding Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** People Operations

## Resignation

Employees resigning from their position must submit written notice to their manager and People Operations. Notice periods by level:

| Level | Notice Period |
|-------|---------------|
| Individual contributor | 2 weeks |
| Senior / Lead | 4 weeks |
| Manager and above | 6 weeks |

The company may, at its discretion, waive part or all of the notice period and pay in lieu.

## Notice Period

During the notice period, the employee is expected to:
- Continue performing their duties to the same standard
- Participate in handover planning
- Document ongoing work, processes, and contacts to a level agreed with their manager

The company reserves the right to place an employee on garden leave during their notice period while continuing to pay their salary.

## Exit Interview

People Operations will invite the departing employee to an exit interview, typically in the final week. Participation is voluntary. Feedback is aggregated and used to improve the employee experience; individual responses are kept confidential from the employee's manager.

## Equipment Return

All company equipment (laptop, accessories, access cards, etc.) must be returned by the last day of employment. Employees will be invoiced for unreturned equipment at replacement cost.

## System Access

IT will revoke all system access at end of business on the employee's last day, or earlier if required by security policy (e.g., involuntary termination). Employees should ensure personal files are removed from company systems before their last day; the company is not responsible for personal data left on company devices.

## Final Pay

The final pay run includes:
- Salary up to and including the last day
- Accrued but unused annual leave (paid out at the daily base rate)
- Any approved outstanding expense claims submitted before departure

Final pay is processed in the next regular payroll cycle following the last day.

## References

People Operations provides standard employment verification references (dates of employment, job title) upon written request. Line managers may provide personal references in a personal capacity, clearly distinguished from official company references.
