# Employee Onboarding Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** People Operations

## Purpose

This policy ensures every new employee has a consistent, structured start that gives them the context, tools, and relationships they need to contribute effectively.

## Pre-boarding (Before Day 1)

People Operations is responsible for:
- Sending a welcome email with first-day logistics at least three business days before start date.
- Provisioning accounts (email, HR portal, Slack, code repositories) so they are ready on day one.
- Shipping or arranging collection of equipment at least two business days before start date.
- Assigning an onboarding buddy from the same or an adjacent team.

The hiring manager is responsible for:
- Preparing a 30/60/90-day plan and sharing it with the new hire in the welcome email.
- Scheduling intro meetings with key stakeholders in the first two weeks.

## Week 1

| Day | Activity |
|-----|-----------|
| Day 1 | IT setup, HR portal orientation, team introductions, office tour |
| Day 2 | Benefits enrollment (must be completed within 5 business days) |
| Day 3 | Compliance training: Code of Conduct, Data Privacy, Security Awareness |
| Day 4–5 | Role-specific tool setup and initial project briefing |

## 30/60/90-Day Milestones

**30 days:** New hire has completed all mandatory compliance training, understands team processes, and has made a first meaningful contribution.

**60 days:** New hire is operating independently on assigned tasks and has completed a first check-in with their manager using the onboarding check-in template.

**90 days:** Formal onboarding closes. Manager and new hire complete the 90-day review form in the HR portal. Any unresolved gaps are documented with an action plan.

## Onboarding Buddy

Each new hire is paired with an onboarding buddy for their first 60 days. The buddy's role is informal: answer day-to-day questions, make social introductions, and help the new hire navigate company norms. Buddies are not responsible for performance management.

## Mandatory Training

The following must be completed within the first 10 business days:
- Code of Conduct
- Data Privacy and GDPR Awareness
- Information Security Basics
- Anti-Harassment and Inclusion

Completion is tracked automatically in the Learning Management System. Failure to complete within the deadline will be escalated to the new hire's manager.
