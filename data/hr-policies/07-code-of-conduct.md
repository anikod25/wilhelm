# Code of Conduct

**Effective date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Legal & People Operations

## Our Commitment

We are committed to maintaining a workplace where every person is treated with dignity and respect. This Code of Conduct applies to all employees, contractors, and anyone acting on the company's behalf — in the office, while travelling on business, at company events, and in online interactions related to work.

## Expected Behaviours

- **Integrity:** Be honest in all professional dealings. Do not misrepresent your work, qualifications, or the company.
- **Respect:** Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with courtesy regardless of role, background, or opinion.
- **Collaboration:** Share information openly, give credit generously, and resolve disagreements constructively.
- **Accountability:** Take ownership of your work and its outcomes. Raise problems early; don't hide mistakes.
- **Confidentiality:** Protect proprietary information, customer data, and colleague details as outlined in the Data Privacy Policy.

## Prohibited Conduct

The following behaviours are grounds for disciplinary action up to and including termination:

- Harassment, bullying, or intimidation of any kind (see Anti-Harassment Policy)
- Discrimination based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic
- Dishonesty, fraud, or falsification of records
- Misappropriation of company or client assets
- Violations of data protection laws or company security policies
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith

## Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any situation where personal interests could conflict with the company's interests — including outside employment, financial stakes in clients or competitors, and personal relationships with direct reports or vendors. Disclosures are made to your manager and People Operations using the Conflict of Interest Declaration Form.

## Reporting Concerns

Concerns about potential violations can be reported to:
- Your manager or skip-level manager
- People Operations (people@company.com)
- The anonymous Ethics Hotline (details on the intranet)

The company does not tolerate retaliation against employees who report concerns in good faith.

## Consequences

Violations are investigated promptly and impartially. Outcomes depend on the severity of the violation and may include a formal warning, performance improvement plan, suspension, or termination.
