# Expense Reimbursement Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** Finance & People Operations

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees incurring business expenses on behalf of the company. Personal expenses are not reimbursable under any circumstances.

## Submission Deadlines

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Reports submitted after 60 days will not be reimbursed without written approval from the CFO.

## Pre-Approval Requirements

The following require written pre-approval from the employee's manager before the expense is incurred:

- Any single expense over $500
- International travel
- Conference registrations over $300
- Software or subscription purchases

## Travel

**Flights:** Economy class for all flights under 6 hours. Business class is permitted for flights of 6 hours or more, subject to manager approval.

**Hotels:** Up to $250/night in standard cities; up to $350/night in high-cost cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Zurich, Tokyo). Exceptions require VP-level approval.

**Ground transport:** Rideshare or taxi is reimbursable when public transit is impractical. Personal car use is reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate.

**Meals while travelling:** Up to $75/day, excluding alcohol. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Meals and Entertainment

Business meals with clients or prospects are reimbursable up to $100/person. The expense report must include the names and company affiliations of all attendees and the business purpose.

Internal team meals are reimbursable up to $40/person and require manager approval.

## Receipts

Original itemised receipts are required for all expenses over $25. Credit card statements are not acceptable as sole documentation.

## Reimbursement Timeline

Approved expense reports are processed within 10 business days and paid via the employee's regular payroll or direct bank transfer.

## Non-Reimbursable Items

- Alcohol (in any context)
- Personal entertainment, mini-bar charges, in-flight Wi-Fi for personal use
- Traffic or parking fines
- Expenses for family members or guests not on company business
- Clothing or personal grooming
