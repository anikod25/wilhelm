# Learning & Development Policy

**Effective date:** January 1, 2024  
**Owner:** People Operations

## Philosophy

We invest in employee growth because skilled, curious people build better products. Every employee is expected to spend time learning — not just when directed, but as an ongoing professional habit.

## Annual Learning Budget

Each full-time employee receives an annual learning budget of **$1,500** to spend on:

- Online courses and learning platforms (Coursera, Pluralsight, O'Reilly, etc.)
- Books and technical resources
- Conferences and workshops (registration fee only; travel and accommodation are covered separately under the Expense Reimbursement Policy if the event is approved)
- Professional certifications and exam fees

Part-time employees receive a pro-rated budget based on contracted hours.

The budget resets on January 1 each year and does not roll over.

## Approval Process

Purchases under $200 can be made and expensed without prior approval. Purchases between $200 and $1,500 require manager approval via the HR portal. Requests above the annual budget cap require VP-level approval and a documented business case.

## Study Leave

Employees sitting a professional certification exam are entitled to one paid study day per exam, up to a maximum of three study days per calendar year. Study days must be approved by the manager at least five business days in advance.

## Internal Learning

- A library of internal courses is available on the Learning Management System (LMS).
- Employees are encouraged to share knowledge by running lunch-and-learn sessions. Facilitators receive a $50 voucher per session delivered.
- Mentorship pairings are available through the HR portal. Both mentors and mentees are expected to commit to at least one hour per month for a minimum six-month engagement.

## Training Repayment

If the company funds external training or a certification costing over $500 and the employee leaves within 12 months of completing it, a pro-rated repayment applies:

- Departure within 6 months: 100% repayment
- Departure between 6 and 12 months: 50% repayment
- Departure after 12 months: no repayment

Repayment does not apply if the employee is made redundant.
