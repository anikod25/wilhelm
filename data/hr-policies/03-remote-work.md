# Remote Work Policy

**Effective date:** March 1, 2024  
**Owner:** People Operations

## Eligibility

Remote work arrangements are available to employees whose roles do not require a consistent physical presence (e.g., on-site lab work, hardware maintenance, front-desk roles). Eligibility is determined by the employee's manager in consultation with People Operations.

## Hybrid Schedule

The default arrangement is hybrid: employees are expected to be in the office a minimum of two days per week. Specific anchor days (days when whole teams are expected in-person) are set by department heads and communicated at least two weeks in advance.

## Fully Remote Arrangements

Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the employee's department head and People Operations. Requests should be submitted using the Remote Work Request Form and will be reviewed within ten business days.

## Equipment and Workspace

The company provides a standard laptop and peripherals. Employees are responsible for maintaining a safe, distraction-free workspace and a reliable internet connection (minimum 25 Mbps download / 10 Mbps upload recommended).

A one-time home office stipend of $500 is available to employees on approved fully remote arrangements. Claims must be submitted within 90 days of approval.

## Security

Remote employees must:
- Connect to company systems via the approved VPN.
- Not work from public Wi-Fi without VPN active.
- Lock their screen whenever stepping away.
- Store all company data in approved cloud storage, not on local drives.

## Availability

Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core hours (10:00 AM – 4:00 PM in their local time zone) and to attend all scheduled meetings with camera on unless technical issues prevent it.

## Review

Remote work arrangements are reviewed annually or whenever the employee's role or performance status changes significantly.
