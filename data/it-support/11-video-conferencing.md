# Video Conferencing Guide

**Category:** Collaboration Tools  
**Last updated:** January 2024  
**Owner:** IT Helpdesk

## Supported Platforms

The company standard video conferencing tool is **Zoom**. Google Meet is available as a secondary option for quick calls where all participants have Google Workspace accounts. Do not use personal Zoom accounts for business meetings.

## Getting Started with Zoom

Zoom is pre-installed on all company laptops. To activate:

1. Open Zoom and click **Sign In with SSO**
2. Enter the company domain: **company.zoom.us**
3. Sign in with your company email — no separate Zoom password is needed

Your account is automatically provisioned with a Pro licence on day one.

## Scheduling a Meeting

- From Zoom desktop: click **New Meeting** (instant) or **Schedule** (future)
- From Google Calendar: click **Add video conferencing → Zoom** when creating an event
- All meetings should use **Unique meeting IDs** — avoid using your Personal Meeting ID for recurring calls with external guests

## Meeting Security

- Enable the **Waiting Room** for any meeting with external participants
- Set a **passcode** for sensitive meetings (board calls, customer negotiations, disciplinary proceedings)
- Do not share meeting links publicly — send directly to invitees
- Lock the meeting once all expected participants have joined (Security → Lock Meeting)

## In-Office Meeting Rooms

All meeting rooms are bookable via Google Calendar. To connect a room display to Zoom:

1. Book the room in Google Calendar and add a Zoom link
2. In the room, tap **Join** on the room display and enter the meeting ID or passcode
3. Use the room's built-in camera and microphone — do not use your laptop audio at the same time (causes echo)

If the room display is unresponsive, restart it using the power button at the back of the panel, or submit a ticket.

## Troubleshooting Audio/Video

| Issue | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Others cannot hear you | Check Zoom settings: Audio → ensure correct microphone is selected |
| Echo on the call | One participant likely has audio on twice — mute laptop speakers |
| Video is frozen or pixelated | Check internet speed; switch to cable if on Wi-Fi; lower video quality in settings |
| "You are muted" — cannot unmute | The host has muted you; use the chat to notify them |
| Zoom won't open | Force quit and reopen; if persistent, reinstall from intranet.company.com/it/software |

## Recording Meetings

Meetings may be recorded for legitimate business purposes (training, note-taking for absentees). Before recording:

- Inform all participants and get verbal consent
- Do not record meetings with external participants without their explicit consent
- Recordings are stored in the company Zoom cloud account for 90 days, then deleted unless saved to Drive
- Recordings containing confidential information must not be shared outside the intended audience
