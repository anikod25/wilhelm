# Printer and Peripheral Setup Guide

**Category:** Office Equipment  
**Last updated:** January 2024  
**Owner:** IT Helpdesk

## Office Printers

### Finding a Printer

All office printers are listed at **intranet.company.com/it/printers** with floor maps. Printers are labelled with their network name (e.g., PRN-F2-NW for Floor 2, North Wing).

### Adding a Printer (macOS)

1. Open **System Settings → Printers & Scanners**
2. Click **+** to add a printer
3. Select the **IP** tab
4. Enter the printer's IP address (listed on the printer label and the intranet page)
5. Protocol: **AirPrint** or **IPP**
6. Click **Add** — the driver installs automatically

### Adding a Printer (Windows)

1. Open **Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners**
2. Click **Add device** and wait for the printer to appear, or click **Add manually**
3. Select **Add a printer using an IP address or hostname**
4. Enter the printer's IP address and follow the prompts

If the printer does not appear or produces errors, submit a ticket at helpdesk.company.com.

### Printing Securely

Confidential documents should use **Secure Print**:
- In the print dialog, select **Secure Print** and set a PIN
- Walk to the printer, tap your access card or enter the PIN to release the job
- Uncollected secure print jobs are deleted after 2 hours

Do not leave printed documents containing sensitive information unattended at the printer.

## Monitors

Office-based employees are allocated one external monitor. A second monitor requires manager approval and is subject to availability.

To request a monitor, submit a ticket with:
- Your desk location
- Monitor size preference (24" or 27" are standard)

IT will arrange delivery and setup within two business days.

## Webcams and Headsets

A webcam and headset are available on request for any employee. Submit a ticket at helpdesk.company.com with category **Peripherals – Audio/Video**. Standard models are dispatched within one business day.

If you need a specialist headset (noise-cancelling, wireless, or conference-room grade), include the specification and manager approval.

## Docking Stations

Docking stations for office desks can be requested via a helpdesk ticket. IT will configure and test the dock before deployment to ensure compatibility with your laptop model.

## Returning Peripherals

When leaving the company or vacating a desk, return all company peripherals to IT (Floor 2 equipment room). Do not leave equipment at the desk for the next person — unclaimed equipment is not reliably returned to IT inventory.
