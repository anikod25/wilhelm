# Password Reset Guide

**Category:** Account Access  
**Last updated:** January 2024  
**Owner:** IT Helpdesk

## Self-Service Reset (Recommended)

If you have previously registered your phone number or recovery email with the identity provider, you can reset your own password without contacting IT.

1. Go to **account.company.com/reset**
2. Enter your company email address and click **Send code**
3. Enter the 6-digit code sent to your registered mobile number or recovery email
4. Choose a new password that meets the requirements below
5. Click **Save** — your new password is active immediately

If the code does not arrive within two minutes, check your spam folder or click **Resend**. Codes expire after 10 minutes.

## Password Requirements

- Minimum 14 characters
- At least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, and one number
- Cannot match any of your last 10 passwords
- Must not contain your name or email address
- Special characters are encouraged but not required

## Helpdesk-Assisted Reset

If you cannot complete a self-service reset (e.g., you have lost access to your registered phone and recovery email):

1. Submit a ticket at **helpdesk.company.com** or call the IT Helpdesk at ext. 4357 (HELP)
2. You will be asked to verify your identity with your employee ID and manager's name
3. A temporary password will be issued via your personal email on file with HR
4. You must change the temporary password on first login — it expires after 24 hours

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) Reset

If you have lost your MFA device:
- Contact the IT Helpdesk directly — MFA resets cannot be done self-service
- You will need manager approval for an MFA bypass during the reset window
- IT will re-enrol your new device within one business hour during business hours (8 AM–6 PM)

## After a Reset

- All active sessions are terminated when a password is changed
- You will need to sign in again on all devices and applications
- If you are locked out of a specific application after a reset, try signing out and back in before contacting IT
