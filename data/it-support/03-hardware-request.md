# Hardware Request Process

**Category:** Equipment  
**Last updated:** January 2024  
**Owner:** IT Helpdesk

## Standard Equipment

All new employees receive a standard equipment package on their first day (or shipped ahead of time for remote employees). The standard package includes:

- Laptop (MacBook Pro 14" or Dell XPS 15, assigned based on role)
- USB-C hub / docking station
- Wired keyboard and mouse
- Laptop bag or sleeve
- Power adapter

Monitors are provided for office-based employees. Remote employees may request a monitor under the Remote Work Policy's home office stipend.

## Requesting Additional or Replacement Equipment

### Replacement (Broken or Faulty Equipment)

1. Submit a ticket at **helpdesk.company.com** with category **Hardware – Replacement**
2. Describe the fault clearly (e.g., "screen has horizontal lines", "keyboard keys unresponsive")
3. IT will attempt remote diagnostics first; if the device needs to be replaced you will receive a loan unit within one business day
4. Faulty devices must be returned to IT within five business days of receiving the replacement

### Additional Peripherals

Requests for additional peripherals (second monitor, standing desk adapter, ergonomic keyboard, headset, etc.) should be submitted with:
- The specific item and reason
- Manager approval (cc your manager on the ticket or note that you have their verbal approval)

Standard peripherals (up to $150) are approved by IT directly. Items over $150 require Finance sign-off in addition to manager approval.

### Specialist Hardware (Developers, Designers)

Engineers or designers needing higher-spec machines (e.g., additional RAM, GPU for local ML workloads) should submit a ticket with:
- The technical requirement and justification
- Manager approval
- Department head approval for items over $2,000

Requests are reviewed within three business days.

## Upgrade Cycle

Laptops are refreshed on a four-year cycle. Employees approaching the end of the cycle will be contacted by IT six weeks before their device reaches four years. Early upgrades are only approved for documented hardware failure.

## Lost or Stolen Equipment

Report lost or stolen equipment **immediately** to:
1. IT Security — security@company.com (to trigger remote wipe)
2. Your manager
3. Facilities/Security if the theft occurred on-site

Do not delay reporting — remote wipe must be triggered as quickly as possible. File a police report if the device was stolen outside the office; provide the report number to IT.
