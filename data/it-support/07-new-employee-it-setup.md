# New Employee IT Setup Checklist

**Category:** Onboarding  
**Last updated:** January 2024  
**Owner:** IT Helpdesk

## Before You Start

Your IT accounts and laptop will be ready on your first day. If anything is missing, contact the IT Helpdesk at helpdesk.company.com or call ext. 4357.

## Day 1 Checklist

### 1. Collect Your Equipment
- Pick up your laptop from IT (office employees) or confirm your shipped equipment has arrived (remote employees)
- Check the box contains: laptop, power adapter, USB-C hub, keyboard, mouse

### 2. Sign In to Your Laptop
- Power on the laptop — it will be pre-enrolled in device management
- Sign in with the temporary credentials emailed to your personal address by People Operations
- You will be prompted to set a new password on first login (see Password Policy for requirements)

### 3. Set Up MFA
- After setting your password, go to **account.company.com/security**
- Enrol your primary MFA method (authenticator app recommended)
- Enrol a backup method and save your backup codes in the password manager
- See the MFA Setup Guide for full instructions

### 4. Install the VPN Client
- Go to **intranet.company.com/it/vpn** and download the client for your OS
- Sign in and verify you can connect before leaving the setup area
- See the VPN Access Guide for full instructions

### 5. Access Core Applications
All of the following should be available in your browser or app launcher. If any are missing, submit a ticket:

| Application | Access method |
|-------------|---------------|
| Email (Gmail) | mail.google.com |
| Calendar | calendar.google.com |
| Drive | drive.google.com |
| Slack | Desktop app (download from intranet) |
| Zoom | Desktop app (pre-installed) |
| HR portal | hr.company.com |
| Helpdesk | helpdesk.company.com |
| Password manager (1Password) | Pre-installed; activate with your company email |

### 6. Set Up Your Password Manager
- Open 1Password and sign in with your company email
- Import or create entries for any systems you access
- Never save passwords in your browser — use 1Password only

### 7. Enable Disk Encryption
- **macOS:** FileVault is enabled by default on company devices. Confirm it is on: System Settings → Privacy & Security → FileVault
- **Windows:** BitLocker is enabled by default. Confirm: Settings → System → Storage → BitLocker

If encryption is not enabled, contact IT immediately — do not use the device for work until this is resolved.

## Getting Help

For any setup issues on day one, come to the IT helpdesk desk (Floor 2, near the lifts) or call ext. 4357. Remote employees should use the helpdesk chat widget at helpdesk.company.com.
